Privacy Policy for Quick Differ

Effective Date: 01-Jan-2025

At Quick Differ ("we," "our," or "us"), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle your information while you use our online tools, including our HTML compiler and code comparison features (collectively referred to as the "Service"). By accessing or using Quick Differ, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information when you use Quick Differ. You can access all of our tools—such as our HTML compiler, preview feature, and code diff viewer—without creating an account, logging in, or providing any personal data.

We do not track what you type, upload, or compare using our tools. All interactions happen directly in your browser and are never sent to our servers.

2. Advertising and Google Ads

In order to keep Quick Differ free and accessible to everyone, we display advertisements on our website using the Google AdSense program. These ads may use cookies to show you general content based on your browsing behavior across the web—not based on anything you do on our site.

We do not share any user input, code, or activity from Quick Differ with Google or any third parties. We do not personalize ads based on the content you generate or interact with on our platform.

Google's Privacy Practices:

For more information on how Google uses data from partner websites, please refer to their privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps.

Opting Out of Personalized Ads:

If you prefer not to see personalized ads, you can manage your preferences through Google's Ad Settings: adssettings.google.com. You may also disable cookies in your browser, though this may affect the display or relevance of ads.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We do not use cookies for any custom tracking or analytics. Cookies that may appear on the site are used solely by third-party ad providers (such as Google) to help serve relevant ads based on general web behavior.

You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings. Please note that this may affect how ads are shown on our site but will not affect your ability to use the core features of our tools.

4. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data, there is no risk of data exposure from our side. All code input and outputs are handled directly in your browser and are never uploaded, stored, or logged by Quick Differ.

While we do our best to maintain a secure browsing environment, we encourage users to avoid sharing sensitive or private code through any online tools unless they are confident in the environment and content.

5. Third-Party Links

Quick Differ may include links to third-party websites (such as documentation or external tools). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party sites. We recommend reviewing their policies before interacting with them.

6. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in legal requirements or our services. If we make any material changes, we will update the "Effective Date" above and publish the revised policy on this page.

We encourage users to revisit this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your data while using Quick Differ, please don't hesitate to contact us. Your trust is important to us, and we’re happy to clarify anything.
